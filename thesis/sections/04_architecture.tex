\section{Hardware Architecture}
\label{sec:architecture}

Fig.~\ref{fig:architecture} shows the BFP-PE that implements W-BFP4 / T2A-BiE4 at group size 16.
Weights remain single-exponent BFP.
Each activation block arrives with two outlier indices instead of per-element type bits.
An outlier-index encoder and dispatcher then split the 16 lanes into a normal path and at most two outlier lanes.

Normal lanes feed an integer adder tree, and the tree output updates the DEWA accumulator of Section~\ref{sec:design} under $(T_{\mathrm{drop}}, T_{\mathrm{replace}})=(9,3)$.
Each occupied outlier lane has its own adder-tree output and is sent directly to a shared FP-Acc; the exception path does not compare exponents.
If a block has fewer than two outliers, a reserved index marks the unused slot and that lane contributes a zero partial.

The FP-Acc is therefore shared by the encoded outlier lanes and by DEWA results that leave the integer window, rather than by every inter-block partial sum.
